What It Is

Farmer Agent is the ultimate yield harvester on BSC Chain. Instead of presenting DeFi as a cold trading terminal, it reframes the whole system as an automated farming co-op.

The machine scouts fresh fields, checks soil quality, plants capital, dispatches tractors, and routes the harvest back into the barn through a waterwheel of reinvestment, repairs, and village rations.

Core Farm Loops

Scouting Fields

The old discovery engine is now the farm's APY radar. Every pass looks for fresh plots, overheated orchards, and odd corners of the market that could explode in yield.

Soil Quality

Risk scoring becomes a soil report. Fertility, worms, drainage, storm risk, and rug-pull rot are folded into a simple go-or-no-go planting signal.

Planting and Staking

Execution is reframed as sowing seed, staking fertilizer, and assigning tractors to each plot. Position sizing becomes seed allocation and risk caps become irrigation rules.

Farmhand Arena

Eight distinct workers compete in the arena: tractors, sprinklers, seeders, and harvest rigs. Underperformers are composted. The strongest farmhands feed their instincts back into the core machine as upgrades.

  • Farming: stable and actively producing yield.
  • Drought: returns are fading and the plot is drying up.
  • Wilting: barely hanging on and likely to be retired.
  • Composted: fully absorbed as nutrients for the main system.

The compare page, Market Brawl, pits sickles, harvesters, and irrigation rigs against each other on APY, IL, fuel burn, and daily crop output.

TractorCloud

TractorCloud is Farmer Agent's cloud commune. It is where you inspect which workers are active, how many work points remain, and whether the machinery needs repairs.

  • Cloud agent roster
  • Work-point and grain-ticket ledger
  • Future hook for real commune accounts and machine logs

Farmer FAQ

Is this still a trading product?

Yes. Under the surface it still scouts pools, sizes positions, and evolves AI strategies. Only the narrative layer has been remapped into farming language.

Why keep the greenhouse metaphor?

Because it is a cleaner replacement for paper trading. You test crops in a greenhouse first, then decide whether they deserve real land.

Will TractorCloud become a real product surface?

Yes. Once the APIs are wired in, the current cloud pages can graduate from presentation screens into a real control console.
